---
title: Glottal source
gloss: "The buzz from the vibrating vocal folds — a harmonic comb carrying pitch and voice quality, but no vowel."
aka: [glottal flow, "the source"]
series: "Speech from first principles"
---

The stream of air puffs produced by the vocal folds opening and closing ~100 times a second: a *volume-velocity* waveform, and on its own a buzz rather than a vowel. Two properties matter downstream.

Its spectrum is a **comb** — energy only at multiples of the fundamental f₀, the spacing carrying the pitch. And its envelope is **smooth and boring**, falling at about −12 dB/octave, set mostly by how abruptly the folds slam shut. What it does *not* carry is any structure distinguishing "ah" from "ee": that lives entirely in the [vocal tract]({{ '/notes/vocal-tract/' | relative_url }}).

This clean division of labour is the content of the [source–filter model]({{ '/notes/source-filter-model/' | relative_url }}), and it is why a recogniser can afford to discard the source and keep only the [formant]({{ '/notes/formant/' | relative_url }}) envelope. Fricatives break it — their source is turbulence at a constriction partway up the tube, not a periodic glottal buzz.
